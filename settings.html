About LiftLog

Version: 1.0.0

Developer: Gabby

LiftLog is your personal training companion built to help you plan workouts, track every session, and stay consistent over time.

Log sets, reps, and weights, monitor progress, and keep your fitness goals clear and achievable, day after day.

Whether you’re building strength, improving endurance, or forming a daily habit, LiftLog is here to support your journey.
